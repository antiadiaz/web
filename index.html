About my art

Instagram (@antiadiaz)

My work moves across two distinct yet complementary visual voices. On one hand, I create black-and-white graphic humor strips built around bold ink linework, expressive slapstick timing, exaggerated gestures, and hand-lettered dialogue; on the other, I craft atmospheric editorial illustrations and character designs defined by stylized proportions, moody nocturnal palettes, and painterly light and shadow.

I share my comic strips and illustrations with an Instagram community of over 50,000 followers, and I am the author and illustrator of the humor and illustration book “El extraterrestre humano”, published by Penguin Random House. Alongside editorial publishing, I create and self-publish paranormal-themed fanzines and have hands-on experience presenting my artwork at illustration fairs. For commercial commissions, I have worked as an illustrator for brands such as El Corte Inglés, Platanomelón, and La Casa de las Carcasas—designing a collection of five illustrated phone cases for the latter—among other companies.

My creative path is grounded in formal visual arts, graphic design, and motion: I earned a Technical Degree in Illustration (2006–2008) and a Degree in Graphic Design (2008–2011) at EASD Antonio Faílde in Ourense, later specializing through a Character Design course at Güiken School (2018) with Pakoto Martínez, Julio Serrano, and Mr. Mol, a Master's in Illustration & Animation at ESDESIGN Barcelona (2023), and a Traditional 2D Animation course with Santi Riscos (2024).

High-contrast ink strips, comedic pacing, and recurring characters for 50K+ followers (@antiadiaz), the illustrated book El extraterrestre humano (Penguin Random House), and self-published paranormal fanzines presented at illustration fairs.

Atmospheric narrative scenes, character development, painterly shading, and expressive staging, refined with Güiken School's Character Design training (Pakoto Martínez, Julio Serrano & Mr. Mol, 2018).

Custom commercial illustration for El Corte Inglés, Platanomelón, and other brands, plus a five-piece illustrated phone case collection designed for La Casa de las Carcasas.
